ⓘ About the League
Rules & Scoring

League Format

  • DraftKings NFL contests during the regular season
  • Weekly lineups submitted on DraftKings
  • Points based on DraftKings scoring
  • Standings tracked throughout the season
  • Year-end champion crowned after Week 18

Scoring & Stats

  • Total Points = season total across contests
  • Avg / Week = average score per contest
  • Weekly Wins = first-place finishes
  • Best Week = highest single-contest score
  • Earnings = total league winnings recorded
